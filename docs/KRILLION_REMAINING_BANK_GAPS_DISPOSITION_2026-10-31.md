## Why missing 100 is often not a data bug

Rarity is specific to player choices and the original private server's scoring algorithm. A valid answer has no mathematical requirement to score 100. Third-party archives disagree: the 27 July 2026 Spanish-language-official-country question has 20 accepted answers and no documented 100 in one archived snapshot, while another hints site suggests Western Sahara for 100 and a total of 21. Do not grant rarity points based on such contradictory suggestions.
